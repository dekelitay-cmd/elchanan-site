Elchanan

Elchanan is a private personal assistant that runs as a Telegram bot. It is built and used by one person, its owner, for personal productivity: reading and creating calendar events, reading email, and preparing email drafts that the owner approves before anything is sent.

The app is not offered to the public and is not available to other users.

How it uses Google data

  • Google Calendar: lists upcoming events and creates events when the owner asks.
  • Gmail: searches and reads messages when the owner asks, and sends an email only after the owner presses an approval button.
